# Cookie Clicker Clone

This context defines the game language for a browser-based Cookie Clicker-style incremental game. It keeps the player-facing economy terms precise as the implementation grows.

## Language

**Cookie**:
The primary currency the player earns manually and passively.
_Avoid_: Point, coin, score

**Bakery**:
The player's whole cookie-producing economy, including saved progress, buildings, upgrades, achievements, and production rates.
_Avoid_: Account, profile, empire

**Cookie Production**:
The passive rate at which the Bakery generates Cookies over time.
_Avoid_: CPS when used as the canonical term, income, earnings rate

**Click Power**:
The number of Cookies earned from one manual cookie click.
_Avoid_: Tap strength, click value, manual income

**Building**:
A repeatable purchase that increases Cookie Production.
_Avoid_: Generator, unit, producer

**Upgrade**:
A one-time purchase that improves Click Power, Buildings, or Cookie Production.
_Avoid_: Perk, boost, power-up

**Achievement**:
A milestone badge earned by reaching a meaningful Bakery state.
_Avoid_: Trophy, medal, quest

**Offline Progress**:
Cookies awarded for elapsed time while the game was closed, based on the saved Bakery state.
_Avoid_: Background simulation, away earnings
